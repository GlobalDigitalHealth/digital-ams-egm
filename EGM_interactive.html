Evidence & Gap Map - Digital Health Interventions for Antimicrobial Stewardship in Primary Health Care

Digital intervention type × outcome domain. Each cell shows one bubble per confidence level present; the letter inside a bubble is the confidence level (H high, M medium, L low) and the number beside it is the number of studies, so the level can be read without relying on colour. Bubble size also encodes the number of studies. Hover or tab to a cell for the study list; click or press Enter to pin it; press Escape to close. Empty cells are evidence gaps.

Confidence levels are derived from the risk-of-bias appraisal: high confidence = low risk of bias (RoB 2); medium confidence = some concerns (RoB 2); low confidence = high risk of bias (RoB 2) or serious risk of bias (ROBINS-I). Source: systematic review extraction workbook (115 included reports of 108 distinct studies). Keyboard use: Tab moves between cells and filter buttons, Enter or Space opens the study list for the focused cell, Escape closes it. Produced by egm_pipeline_v3.2.R (R) from egm_long_data_v3.csv; the same cell table is drawn as the static map (Figure 2) in the evidence and gap map report.
